Overview

As part of my FOSSEE OpenFOAM internship at IIT Bombay, I am building a 2D, steady-state simulation of a supersonic scramjet inlet with turbulence modeling.

Baseline and validation

I am reproducing the Mach 5 inlet-isolator geometry from Che Idris et al., Sensors 2014, 14, 6606, in OpenFOAM and working toward validation against the paper's results. The model is 135 mm long, measured from the first-ramp leading edge to the isolator exit.

Current work

My workflow starts with geometry and blockMesh, followed by mesh quality checks with checkMesh, solver setup, and validation. I am currently running a mesh independence study with coarse, medium, and fine meshes. I refine the mesh heavily near the intake, where the strongest flow features occur.

Next steps

Once the setup is validated, I plan to use it as a baseline for a design modification, excluding bleed-air geometry. My longer-term goal is to investigate ways to reduce noise. The mesh study and validation are ongoing, and I have not established a performance or noise reduction result.
